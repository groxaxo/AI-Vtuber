TTS technology refers to Text-to-Speech technology, an artificial intelligence technology that converts written text into natural-sounding speech. The principles behind this technology involve deep learning and speech synthesis fields.

Simply put, TTS technology can be divided into the following steps:

1. Text Analysis: First, the system analyzes the input text, identifying words, sentence structure, and grammar rules.

2. Linguistic Processing: Next, based on the analysis results, the system determines the appropriate speech synthesis rules and models, as well as the corresponding speech units from the voice database.

3. Speech Synthesis: In this step, the system uses pre-trained models to convert the text into speech according to the content and linguistic rules.

4. Voice Selection: TTS systems typically offer a variety of voice options, allowing users to choose an appropriate voice according to their preferences or needs.

5. Voice Output: Finally, the system outputs the processed speech, allowing users to hear the converted audio content.

TTS technology has applications in many fields, such as assistive reading, voice assistants, and education and training. Through TTS technology, computers can communicate more intuitively with users, providing more convenient services and experiences.
