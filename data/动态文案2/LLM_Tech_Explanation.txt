LLM technology usually refers to Large Language Models, a technology based on machine learning and natural language processing. This technology works by training large-scale neural network models to understand and generate natural language text.

Large language models typically have billions or even trillions of parameters and are pre-trained on massive text corpora. During the pre-training phase, the model learns the structure, grammar rules, and semantic information of language, enabling it to generate natural and fluent text or understand input text in subsequent tasks.

LLM technology has wide-ranging applications in the field of natural language processing, including machine translation, text summarization, question-answering systems, and language understanding and generation. In recent years, with the continuous development of deep learning technology, large language models have achieved remarkable results in these tasks, such as the GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) series and BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) models.

The advancement of LLM technology has brought many new possibilities to the field of natural language processing, enabling computers to better understand and generate natural language, thus offering more possibilities for human-computer interaction and information processing.
